Are You Single and Searching?

You're not alone.

Many Christian women long for marriage, but feel confused, discouraged, or unsure of what to do next.

Are you asking questions like...

  • How do I know if a man is truly right for me?
  • Where can I meet serious Christian men?
  • How can I tell whether a relationship has marriage potential?
  • Am I being too picky—or ignoring important red flags?
  • How do I date intentionally without becoming desperate?
  • Why do I keep attracting the wrong kinds of relationships?
  • How can I prepare myself for marriage while I'm still single?
  • How do I trust God while also taking practical steps toward marriage?

♡ If you've wrestled with questions like these, you're in the right place.
